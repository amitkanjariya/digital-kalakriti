The Artisan Marble · Digital Kalakriti

From hand chisels
to digital design.

For generations, marble artisans have shaped stone by hand. Digital Kalakriti brings that craft together with 3D design, so a temple or sculpture can be planned before the carving begins.

Based in Surat, with roots in Rajasthan’s marble tradition, the team creates temples, murtis and stonework for homes and projects in India and abroad.
